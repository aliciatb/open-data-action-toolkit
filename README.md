# About

The [open data actions and measures toolkit](https://aliciabrown-open-data-action-toolkit.share.connect.posit.cloud) provides guidance on how to measure organizational actions and public engagement with open data to present a cohesive view of the activity and use of open data in a community. The actions and measures shared in the toolkit were identified from case study research of 3 local governments in the U.S. and from a literature review on open data use, participation, engagement, and user-friendly data products.

The examples provided in the toolkit represent the data portals, products, and measures from organizations who participated in the case study research of the City of Austin, the City of Baton Rouge and Parish of East Baton Rouge, and the City and County of San Francisco.
