# About

The [open data actions and measures toolkit](https://aliciabrown-open-data-action-toolkit.share.connect.posit.cloud) provides guidance on how to measure organizational actions and public engagement with open data to present a cohesive view of the activity and use of open data in a community. The actions and measures shared in the toolkit were identified from case study research of 3 local governments in the U.S. and from a literature review on open data use, participation, engagement, and user-friendly data products.

The examples provided in the toolkit are based on the data, data products, and reporting created by case study participants from the City of Austin, the City of Baton Rouge and Parish of East Baton Rouge, and the City and County of San Francisco open data programs. Community developed analysis, tools, and data products are also presented here demonstrating the important role of open data intermediaries to synthesize government data, create innovative tools for exploring data, and advocate on behalf of the public.

## Dashboards

- The [tylertech_catalog_analytics.pbix](https://github.com/aliciatb/open-data-action-toolkit/blob/main/_dashboards/tylertech_catalog_analytics.pbix) Power BI dashboard demonstrates how to query the Tyler Technologies [Discovery API](https://dev.socrata.com/docs/other/discovery#?route=overview--purpose) to query all assets for a data portal and view metadata and analytics for all assets.
- The [zotero_news_article_citations.pbix](https://github.com/aliciatb/open-data-action-toolkit/blob/main/_dashboards/zotero_news_article_citations.pbix) Power BI dashboard demonstrates how citations of articles may be analyzed to learn how open data is being used in the community.

## Data

The [data](https://github.com/aliciatb/open-data-action-toolkit/tree/main/_data) folder contains:
 - 3 text files of article citations used in the [zotero_news_article_citations.pbix](https://github.com/aliciatb/open-data-action-toolkit/blob/main/_dashboards/zotero_news_article_citations.pbix) Power BI dashboard
 - The [tylertech_discovery.xlsx](https://github.com/aliciatb/open-data-action-toolkit/blob/main/_data/tylertech_discovery.xlsx), like the tylertech_catalog_analytics.pbix](https://github.com/aliciatb/open-data-action-toolkit/blob/main/_dashboards/tylertech_catalog_analytics.pbix) Power BI dashboard, demonstrates how to connect to the [Discovery API](https://dev.socrata.com/docs/other/discovery#?route=overview--purpose) using Excel rather than Power BI.
